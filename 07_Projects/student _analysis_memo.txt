To: The Headmaster 
From: Data Analyst 
Re: Student Performance Analysis — Key Findings & Recommendations

Our analysis of 1,000 student records reveals that overall performance is above average with a school-wide 
mean score of approximately 77 out of 100. However three factors are significantly influencing which students 
fall behind.

The single biggest driver of underperformance is family income. Students on free or reduced lunch score 7.66 
points below their peers on average — a gap confirmed statistically to be real and not random. This suggests 
that students from lower income households need targeted support.

The most actionable finding is test preparation. Students who completed a preparation course scored 8 points 
higher on average. Since the school can directly offer and even subsidize this intervention, we recommend making 
test preparation available and free to all students — prioritizing those from lower income backgrounds.

Finally parental education shows a clear pattern — students whose parents have higher education perform better, 
likely due to stronger home support. We recommend introducing a parent engagement program to bring families into 
the learning process regardless of their own educational background.

Summary of recommendations:

Offer free test preparation to all students
Create a targeted support program for students on free or reduced lunch
Launch a parent engagement initiative to strengthen home learning support
Monitor progress of at-risk students termly to track improvement
